Most of this site is about models running in a data centre. Plenty of useful AI runs somewhere else: on a phone, in a car, in a camera, or in a device small enough to sit in your ear. Choosing where inference runs is a design decision with consequences for latency, privacy, cost and battery.

## What pushes toward the device

- **Latency you cannot spend.** Some tasks have budgets of milliseconds. A network round trip alone can exceed them, and it varies from one moment to the next.
- **Connectivity.** The feature has to work in a tunnel, on a plane, or in a building with poor signal.
- **Privacy.** Raw audio, images or health data never leave the device.
- **Cost at scale.** Millions of devices each making frequent calls add up. On-device compute is paid for once, in hardware.
- **Reliability.** No dependency on a service being up.

## What pushes toward the cloud

- **Model size.** Large models do not fit in a device's memory or power budget.
- **Quality.** The biggest models are generally more capable, especially on open-ended tasks.
- **Update speed.** You can change a cloud model today. A device model changes through a firmware or app update.
- **Fleet visibility.** It is easier to monitor, log and A/B test a service than a million devices.
- **Heterogeneous hardware.** One cloud target is easier than many chipsets.

## Hybrid designs

| Pattern | Idea |
|---|---|
| **Device first, cloud fallback** | A small on-device model handles the common case; harder cases go to the cloud when a connection allows |
| **Split by task** | Time-critical work (wake word, noise suppression, safety) on device; heavy work (summaries, search) in the cloud |
| **Cloud-trained, device-deployed** | Train and evaluate in the cloud, compress the model, ship it to the device |
| **Speculative or cascaded** | A device model answers right away and a larger model corrects it later if needed |

## Constraints that are easy to underestimate

- **Power.** Every inference costs battery, and sustained compute causes heat. Measure energy per inference, not only speed.
- **Memory.** A device may have a few megabytes available for your model, not gigabytes.
- **Operator support.** The chip's runtime may not support every operation your model uses, which can force a redesign.
- **Update path.** Plan how models get updated safely in the field, and how to roll back.
- **Worst case, not average.** Real-time features fail on the slow tail. Budget for p99.
