---
title: How News notes are written
date: 2026-10-26
tags: [meta]
---

News is where market changes land: a new model, a platform feature, a pricing move. Each note follows the same shape so it can be read in under two minutes.

- **What changed.** One or two sentences, with the date and a link to the primary source.
- **Why it matters.** The effect on a system you might be building: design, cost, risk.
- **What to do.** Usually "nothing yet", sometimes "re-run your evals" or "check your quota".

Notes are drafted from release notes and papers, then read and edited by a person before they go out. If a number cannot be traced to a source, it does not get published.

Below the notes, the News page also lists **headlines from official feeds** (cloud providers, model labs, serving projects and security groups). That list refreshes by itself once a day. It is not reviewed: each entry is only a title, a date and a link to the source, so use it to see what moved, and read the original before you rely on it.
