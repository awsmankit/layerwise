**What changed.** OWASP's GenAI Security Project published the 2026 edition of its Top 10 for LLM applications, dated 3 August 2026. OWASP describes it as having updated rankings and expanded threat coverage. The 2025 edition led with prompt injection and listed system prompt leakage seventh; check the 2026 guide for what moved.

**Why it matters.** Many security reviews and questionnaires cite these IDs. Rankings changed between editions, so say which edition you mean when you cite an ID.

**What to do.** Map your threat model to the 2026 names, and re-run your prompt-injection tests. The [LLM security lesson](../../learn/evals/llm-security/) walks through each risk.
