# Code of conduct

Layerwise is for learners of every age and background, including children and the adults who teach them. Please keep every space around it (issues, pull requests, discussions) kind, patient and welcoming.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and remember that a beginner's question is a good question.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or spam.
- Keep examples and content suitable for a school audience.
- Never share other people's personal information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, open an issue or message the maintainer, [awsmankit](https://github.com/awsmankit), on GitHub.
